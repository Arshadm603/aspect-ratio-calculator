About Us

Welcome to Aspect Ratio Calculator. Our goal is to provide a simple, free and fast tool for students, designers, photographers, video editors and YouTubers to calculate aspect ratio easily.

Whether you need 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels / TikTok, or 4:3 for old videos, our calculator helps you find the perfect width and height in seconds.

We believe in keeping tools simple and free for everyone.
