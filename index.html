Platform Dimension Guide for Creators

Different social channels demand specific aspect ratios to maximize viewer screen estate:

  • YouTube & Desktop (16:9): The universal layout for standard horizontal videos and presentation displays.
  • TikTok, Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts (9:16): Full-screen vertical orientation designed entirely for mobile phones.
  • Instagram Feed Posts (1:1 or 4:5): Square or slightly taller portrait rectangles that take up more mobile scroll space than standard landscapes.

How the Aspect Ratio Formula Works

To calculate a missing dimension manually, cross-multiplication is used. If you know your target width and your desired aspect ratio, the equation is: Height = (Target Width × Ratio Height) ÷ Ratio Width. This calculator automates that math instantly in your browser.
