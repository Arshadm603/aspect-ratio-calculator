How To Use This Wallpaper Size Calculator? (Step by Step)

Bohat se log Google se koi bhi wallpaper download kar ke laga lete hain, phir wo blur ya kata hua lagta hai. Iski wajah size ka farq hai. Is tool se aap 10 second me sahi size nikal sakte hain:

  1. Step 1: Ooper se apna device select karein. Jaise Android, iPhone ya Laptop.

  3. Step 3: Calculate button dabayein. Aapko perfect size, aspect ratio aur quality tip mil jayegi.
  4. Step 4: Usi size ka wallpaper download karein. Wo bilkul sharp aur full screen lagega.

Why Does My Wallpaper Look Blurry?

Agar aap 720p ki photo ko 1080p ya 4K screen pe lagayenge to wo phat jayegi. Hamesha apni screen se barabar ya us se bara size use karein. Choti photo ko kabhi bara mat karein.

Pro Tip: Mobile ke liye hamesha lambi (portrait) photo lein, PC ke liye chauri (landscape). Ulti photo lagane se adhi photo cut jati hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best wallpaper size for mobile?

Most Android ke liye 1080 x 2400 best hai. iPhone 14/15 ke liye 1179 x 2556 use karein.

What is the best wallpaper size for PC?

Full HD laptop ke liye 1920 x 1080 aur 4K monitor ke liye 3840 x 2160 best hai.

Can I use same wallpaper for phone and PC?

Nahi, phone lamba hota hai aur PC chaura. Dono ke liye alag size chahiye warna crop ho jayega.
